      National Cybercrime Reporting & Coordination

      🏛️ Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C)

      I4C is the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre under the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India.

      Ministry of Home Affairs, Govt. of India

      National Cybercrime Nodal Point

      I4C acts as a nodal point in the fight against cybercrime across India. It coordinates action among central agencies, state police departments, and financial institutions to combat cybercrime in a coordinated and comprehensive manner.

      ⚠️ National Emergency Action for Financial Cyber Fraud:
      Call 1930 immediately.
      Reporting within the Golden Hour (first 1 to 2 hours) enables I4C and banks to track and freeze stolen funds before they are cashed out.
      📍 Official Location: NDCC-II Building, Jai Singh Road, New Delhi - 110001
      📞 National Helpline: 1930 (Toll-Free, 24x7)
      🌐 Official Reporting Portal: cybercrime.gov.in ↗
      🛡️ Official Awareness Campaign: CyberDost (@cyberdosti4c)
