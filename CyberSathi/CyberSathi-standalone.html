🚨 IMMEDIATE GUIDANCE

I Got Scammed — What Should I Do?

CyberSathi provides educational guidance. Official complaints belong on government channels.

1

Stay Calm

Do not panic or continue communicating with the scammer.

2

Contact Your Bank

Immediately inform your bank or payment provider.

3

Secure Accounts

Change compromised passwords and block or freeze cards where appropriate.

4

Save Evidence

Keep screenshots, transaction details, messages, URLs, phone numbers and emails. Do not share credentials publicly.

5

Report

For financial cyber fraud, call 1930 and use the official portal.

Report Cyber Crime ↗
6

Tell a Trusted Person

Involve a trusted family member or responsible person so you are not handling the situation alone.
