      Our Mission

      Why CyberSathi?

      Technology should create opportunities, not fear. As digital payments and mobile banking reach every rural household, cyber fraudsters increasingly target the vulnerable. CyberSathi bridges the knowledge gap with plain-language, relatable education.

      🌾

      Village-Centric Language

      No complicated technical jargon. We explain threats in English, Hindi, and Marathi using relatable village scenarios like mandi payments, crop insurance, and pension calls.

      🗣️

      Voice Saathi Accessibility

      Designed for elders and non-literate digital users. Simply tap the microphone to ask questions by voice and receive spoken advice in your preferred language.

      🤝

      Volunteer Brigade

      Training college students and youth to act as trusted cyber safety companions for their own gram panchayats, cooperative banks, and families.

      ⚡

      Golden Hour Protection

      Clear step-by-step guidance on what to do in the critical first 2 hours after a scam: contacting your bank, preserving evidence, and calling 1930.
