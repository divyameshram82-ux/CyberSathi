Ministry of Home Affairs Initiative

Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C)

I4C acts as a nodal point in the fight against cybercrime across India. For financial fraud, reporting within the Golden Hour to helpline 1930 enables authorities to coordinate with banks to freeze stolen funds before they are withdrawn.

Official Helpline: 1930 (Toll-Free, 24x7) • Online Reporting: cybercrime.gov.in ↗
Visit Official cybercrime.gov.in ↗
Important Clarification: CyberSathi is a grassroots community cyber-safety educational project. 1930 is the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Helpline operated by the Ministry of Home Affairs (I4C), Government of India. In cases of financial cyber fraud, dial 1930 and report on cybercrime.gov.in immediately.
